# Phases & Milestones

*Cross-reference: See `prd.md` for detailed product requirements and scope.*

## Phase 1: MVP
**Goal**: Validate the core three-sided marketplace in a single local market.

### Scope:
- [ ] Customer registration & location
- [ ] Restaurant listing & search (normal)
- [ ] Nearby/map food discovery (P0)
- [ ] Restaurant/home-kitchen onboarding (P0)
- [ ] Restaurant menu, cart, checkout, payment
- [ ] Razorpay integration (P0)
- [ ] Coupons/offers (P0)
- [ ] Order management (restaurant side)
- [ ] Basic delivery workflow
- [ ] Restaurant Partner Panel (core)
- [ ] Delivery Partner Panel (core)
- [ ] Admin Panel (core: approvals, orders, users)
- [ ] Notifications (order lifecycle)
- [ ] Ratings & reviews

## Phase 2
**Goal**: Improve retention, differentiation, and provide advanced ordering options.

### Scope:
- [ ] AI Food Assistant & AI item-level search (P1)
- [ ] Natural language search
- [ ] Tastifyy Coins
- [ ] Referral / Share & Earn
- [ ] Group order + split bill (P1)
- [ ] Scheduled orders (P1)
- [ ] Last-Minute Deals (P1)
- [ ] Restaurant analytics
- [ ] Order-history recommendations (P1)
- [ ] Price comparison/cheap alternatives (P1)
- [ ] Birthday benefits (P1)
- [ ] Quick-response support (P1)

## Phase 3
**Goal**: Scale, advanced AI, and new business models.

### Scope:

- [ ] Advanced AI personalization (P2)
- [ ] Community competitions
- [ ] Premium membership
